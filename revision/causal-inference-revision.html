Compressed from the decision tree in the notes. Use it to recall branch logic, then read the full framework and table there.

Do you have randomization?
↓
Yes Full compliance → standard A/B effect. Non-compliance → IV / CACE.
No Observational: branch on panel vs cross-section and cutoff vs no cutoff.
↓
Observational: repeated outcomes over time?
↓
Many donors / aggregate Synthetic control when a good donor pool exists.
Treated + control groups DiD when parallel trends are plausible.
↓
Single unit, many periods Interrupted time series.
Cross-section RDD if sharp cutoff; else PSM/IPW if confounders observed; IV if credible instrument.

Personalization layers (HTE, uplift) sit on top of a valid base design.

4. Assumptions — revision strip

Ultra-compressed; the notes and each method README spell out checks, threats, and alternatives.

Method What you are leaning on
PSM / IPWUnconfoundedness, positivity/overlap, reasonably well-specified propensity.
DiDParallel trends (counterfactual path); clean pre-period; watch staggered timing.
Synthetic controlPre-treatment fit predicts counterfactual; limited spillover/interference.
ITSCorrect dynamic model; no major co-interventions; stable seasonality.
IVRelevance + exclusion; often LATE not ATE; watch weak instruments.
RDDContinuity / no manipulation at cutoff; interpret locally to bandwidth.
HTE / upliftUnderlying causal design is valid; enough support within segments.
CACEInstrument for uptake; monotonicity; defensible exclusion.

5. Pitfalls and diagnostics

What to say in a review or interview when someone shows a causal slide. Deep dives in notes §6 and in each method README.

Propensity work

  • Calibrate probabilities, not only discrimination.
  • Trim lack of overlap; report who drops.
  • Balance tables / Love plots after matching.

DiD work

  • Plot pre-trends; event-study leads should be ~flat.
  • Know staggered-DiD estimator choices at a high level.

SC & IV

  • SC: placebo pre-periods, leave-one-out donors.
  • IV: first-stage strength, over-ID where possible, LATE wording.

General

  • Predictive importance is not a causal driver list.
  • Sensitivity, placebos, replication where possible.
